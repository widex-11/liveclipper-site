Privacy Policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

LiveClipper is a desktop application that runs on the user's own computer. The developer does not operate a server for it and does not receive, collect or store any data of its users.

Data the application handles on your computer

  • Sign-in tokens for your Google/YouTube and TikTok accounts. They are stored only in the Windows Credential Manager of your computer.
  • Videos and metadata created from streams you choose, stored in folders on your computer.
  • Statistics (for example view and like counts) of the videos you upload, stored in a local database on your computer.

Use of Google user data

The application requests access to your YouTube account only to upload videos you choose and to read the statistics of those videos. LiveClipper's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Data from Google APIs is not shared with anyone, not sold and not used for advertising.

Use of TikTok user data

The application requests access to your TikTok account only to send videos you choose to your TikTok inbox as drafts and to read the basic statistics of your own videos. This data is not shared with anyone and not sold.

Sharing

The developer does not receive any of this data. The application communicates only with the services you connect (Google/YouTube, TikTok) and with Twitch to read public stream and chat data.

Your choices

You can disconnect an account in the application at any time, delete the local data by removing the application folders, and revoke the application's access in your Google account or in your TikTok account settings.

Changes

Changes to this policy are published on this page with a new date.
