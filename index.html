LiveClipper

LiveClipper is a small desktop application for Windows. It records live streams that the user chooses, cuts short vertical highlight clips and lets the user upload them to their own YouTube and TikTok accounts. The application runs entirely on the user's own computer. It is not a hosted service and has no user accounts of its own.

What the app does with your accounts

  • YouTube: uploads videos to the channel you connect and reads basic statistics (views, likes) of those videos.
  • TikTok: sends a video as a draft to the TikTok inbox of the account you connect and reads basic statistics of your own videos.

Access is granted by you through the official sign-in pages of Google and TikTok and can be revoked at any time in your Google or TikTok account settings.
